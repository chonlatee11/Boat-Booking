## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, staff member or pier_admin, **I want to** sign in with a one-time code and (as an admin) manage operators, piers, routes, boats and prices, **so that** customers can browse the catalog publicly while every admin only ever touches their own operator's piers. *(Restatement of the ROADMAP Phase 2 goal.)*

This plan's slice: **As a** customer on my phone, **I want to** sign in with my email or phone number and a one-time code, **so that** my bookings (Phase 4+) are tied to me without a password.
